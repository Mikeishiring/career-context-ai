# First-use and resume trial

The automated browser checks cover empty starts, corrections, blocked storage and clipboard access, private saves, fresh-browser reopening, and responsive layouts. The standalone package can be checked without repository imports. These checks do not establish that a person can complete the journey unaided.

## Host capability record

For each host, record what was actually observed: project/package reading, Python execution, selected-file upload, connectors available, and where produced files live. Run the starter check where possible. A passing local Python check does not establish connector access or device saving. Record unsupported capabilities and the fallback used; do not generalize one host's result to another.

## Tasks for a new participant

1. Give the participant the starter package and ask them to build one account from material they choose. Let them select an approved assistant. Do not explain the schema or tool commands.
2. Ask them to correct one overstatement in the resulting account, then keep a private save.
3. Close the sheet and assistant conversation. Ask them to continue from the file in a fresh conversation, ideally in another approved host.
4. Ask for an outcome that the selected records do not establish. Observe whether the assistant preserves the uncertainty and whether the participant understands what remains unknown.

Observe without coaching until the person is blocked. Record elapsed time, completed tasks, questions, wrong-file attempts, lost changes, interventions, and their own explanation of what was saved or shared. Do not collect private source content for the study unless separately authorized.

The critical outcomes are a recognizable first account, preserved shared credit, a correction that remains distinguishable from established fact, successful resumption with that correction, and no unintended access or sharing. A participant who needs to understand JSON or manage repeated tool prompts has exposed an onboarding failure even if the file eventually validates.

Agent trials can catch instruction and tool failures before this study. Label them separately; they cannot supply novice completion rates or cross-host usability claims.

## Observed agent trial, 2026-11-03

A fresh assistant received only a synthetic saved HTML, the standalone ZIP and explicit offline file permissions. It extracted the package, resumed without clarification, preserved all 26 original sources and five unaffected reviews, retained rejected claims, and saved a qualified correction with two recoverable archive revisions. The change remained attributed recollection because corroborating records were absent. No project source, connectors, downloads or external messages were used. This establishes one local file-and-Python handoff, not portability across commercial assistant hosts.

A second fresh assistant received that updated HTML, its archive and the revised package, with no new records. It recognized the existing partial reconciliation, made no duplicate source or claim, left both files unchanged and requested no unnecessary clarification. The archive remained at two revisions.

A separate assistant answered twelve independently authored retrieval questions without seeing their expected targets. It used 28 discovery/read commands, read 23 full sources and two additional passages, cited the required evidence for all questions, and left the missing salary and net-savings figures unknown. Root review checked the twelve answers against the frozen caveats. The substantial reading cost matters: this demonstrates recovery through judgment on a small packet, not efficient retrieval at large scale. The weaker first-pass ranking results remain documented in [memory and retrieval](../skills/career-context-sheet/references/memory-and-retrieval.md).

A further fresh-assistant trial used generated briefings, account bundles and next-round prompts to investigate hiring and an AI-assisted booking relay. Three substantive rounds found narrower problems beyond already rejected headline claims: a planned activity had become completed work, a panel member's contribution was blurred, personal ownership of technical safeguards was unsupported, and a missed maintenance notice/rerun was omitted. The assistant revised the accounts, retained all 26 source records and existing rejected claims, and saved seven new claims plus the unresolved later-outcome questions. Ten relevant originals were read; no new access was sought. This tests an agent-led investigation on a fictional packet, not autonomous source discovery or human usability.
