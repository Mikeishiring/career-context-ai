About this experiment

What stayed fixed: The candidate, the hiring question, and the 1-page resume bullet never change. Only the context given to the AI—and whether the local L2 confidentiality shutter is closed—changes.

How Career Context AI v2 works on your machine: You store your own raw work records (`L0`) and private stories (`L1`) in a local SQLite database (`career.sqlite`) using your own AI assistant or offline files (PDF, DOCX, Slack, Notion, Calendar, Transcripts). Nothing connects to our servers. When you're ready to share, a deterministic local leak checker blocks registered client names, revenue figures, emails, API keys, and `#channels` before exporting a succinct brief for humans or an exhaustive L2 Context Pack for someone else's AI.

Build your own local career vault

Runs locally on Python 3.11+ with zero required packages. Bring your own AI (MCP or web chat), drop a PDF resume, or try Maya Lin's full interactive Studio sample in 10 seconds.
