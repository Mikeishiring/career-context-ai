What do L0, L1, L2, L3 mean?

Default private (L0/L1); share confidential-safe (L2) or public (L3) only when you choose
L0 · Raw Vault

Everything scanned verbatim

Raw Slack threads, Notion pages, Google Docs, Calendar entries, meeting transcripts, GitHub PRs, and social posts. Indexed locally for millisecond search. Never exported.

L1 · Private Synthesis (Default)

Your candid career database

Your private roles, stories, real customer/coworker names, exact metrics, shelved prototypes, failures, visuals, and verbatim anchored claims—so you never have to censor your own memory.

L2 · Confidential-Safe Overview

For hiring managers & advisors

Concrete problems, decisions, trade-offs, shared credit, and generalized scope (e.g. "a Tier-1 bank" instead of client names). Deterministically leak-checked before saving or sharing.

L3 · Public Highlight

For public profiles & intros

Crisp 2–3 sentence public summaries and approved public links (GitHub, Substack, talks). Zero internal specifics, zero JavaScript, strict leak checking.

Plain-English glossary (MCP, BYO-LLM, SQLite)

What is MCP?

Model Context Protocol is the universal connector layer your AI assistant uses to read your live work tools—Slack, Notion, Google Docs, Calendar, Meeting Transcripts, GitHub, Linear, Gmail—directly, instead of forcing you to export ZIPs by hand. It also lets you expose a read-only, leak-checked L2 view of your vault to another agent.

What is BYO-LLM?

Bring Your Own LLM means Career Context AI has no proprietary cloud or subscription lock-in. Use Claude, ChatGPT/Codex, Gemini, Cursor, Antigravity, or a local Ollama model. Your LLM handles reasoning and storytelling; local Python handles storage, search, gap auditing, and leak blocking.

Why Deepening Radar?

Normally AI stops after reading 6 files and makes you keep asking "what else did I miss?". Our deterministic gap engine tracks unscanned channels, empty roles, long unlinked docs, thin stories, and robotic legal phrasing so both you and your agent keep going deeper automatically.
