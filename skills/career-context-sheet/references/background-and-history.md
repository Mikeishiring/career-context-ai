# Background and prior work

When the available context covers earlier work, include both a career timeline and a short background story. Start at the earliest supported meaningful period, not at the employer with the largest export. Include relevant independent work, physical work, caring, study, travel or breaks when the person has supplied them. Do not fill an empty early history with a generic biography or make a complete CV a prerequisite for a useful account.

The timeline answers what happened and when. Keep roles, organisations, dates and sources together. Use the precision the evidence supports: a multi-year role may remain one period beside later quarterly records. Preserve concurrent roles, uncertain endpoints, gaps and current work; never invent a sequential ladder or infer an end date from the next role's start.

The background story connects the supported settings and transitions in plain language. The person's account can explain why a move mattered. Chronology alone cannot establish motivation, a transferable skill, a lifelong purpose or how an early role shaped later work. Ask a focused question only when that missing meaning matters; the known chronology can stand on its own.

Keep this information in the portable packet: `person.background` for the short narrative, source-linked `timeline` periods for chronology, and background accounts for fuller context. Preserve stable account/source IDs and source text. A display summary is a projection, not a replacement for these records. Full private JSON and AI context exports should retain the earlier history, date precision, shared credit and uncertainty.

Make Background and the full Timeline reachable from the private reading view. Present earlier roles with the same care as recent digital projects, without giving unsupported achievements equal weight. When earlier context is absent, omit the empty section and leave a useful optional lead. Public profile inclusion remains a separate choice.

Check a rich history, a sparse first role, overlapping roles and approximate dates. An earlier period must survive saving and AI export, and open the right account/source without losing later work or the person's corrections.
